Good to Know

What a night holds.

The Evening
Arrive at dusk. A conversation with Jordan, a thirty-minute guided meditation, then two hours of sky watch.
What to Bring
Warm layers, a chair or blanket, and an open mind. The exact location and arrival time are emailed after you reserve.
Twenty Seats
Each night holds twenty seats and confirms once twelve are reserved. If a night doesn’t confirm, you are refunded in full.
If the Sky Closes
These nights live outdoors. If weather closes the sky, your seat moves to a new date or is refunded — your choice.
