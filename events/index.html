Good to Know

What a night holds.

The Evening
Arrive at dusk. A conversation with Jordan, a thirty-minute guided meditation, then two hours of sky watch.
What to Bring
Warm layers, a chair or blanket, and an open mind. The exact location and every detail are emailed a week before your night.
Twenty Seats
Seats are paid in full when you reserve. A night confirms a week ahead, once twelve of its twenty seats are taken. If it doesn’t, you are refunded in full.
If the Sky Closes
These nights live outdoors. If weather closes the sky, your seat moves to a new date or is refunded — your choice.
