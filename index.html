The Invitation

An evening that asks something else of you.

Night Under the Stars is a single evening beneath a dark sky: a small circle, a guided meditation, and two unhurried hours of watching together.

Some come after an anomalous experience of their own. Some have followed the UAP story and the question of non-human intelligence, and are looking for more than headlines. We aren’t out seeking UFOs or craft. We go out seeking a transcendent experience, creating the conditions that have, across many traditions and many lives, made it possible for the phenomenon to show up.
