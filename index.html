The Invitation

An evening that asks something else of you.

Night Under the Stars is a single evening beneath a dark sky: a small circle, a guided meditation, and two unhurried hours of watching together.

Some come after a UFO sighting of their own. Some have followed the UAP story and the question of non-human intelligence, NHI, and want more than headlines. We aren’t out hunting for craft. We go out seeking something transcendent, and sometimes the phenomenon shows up. We never promise that it will.

The Night

One night, one sky.

i

ii

iii
The Meditation
Thirty minutes of guided stillness, practiced together.
iv
The Sky Watch
Two unhurried hours under the open sky. You watch, you listen, you notice.
